What TimeVault does

Less hunting.
More context.

TimeVault keeps the important parts of Time Machine close at hand, in one native macOS interface.

01

Backup status

See the current state.

Quickly tell whether a backup drive is connected, a backup is idle, or access needs attention.

02

Snapshot browser

Browse with your drive in context.

Explore local and mounted snapshots while keeping the active drive and sort order visible.

03

Snapshot comparison

See what changed between two points.

Choose an older and newer snapshot to review additions, removals, modifications, and metadata changes.

Snapshot comparison

Choose two points.
Review the difference.

TimeVault gives the older and newer snapshots clear roles, then lists the changes in between. It does not restore, delete, or alter your backups.
